ShowAroo is built with your privacy in mind. We do not sell or share your personal data. Most of your activity — watch history, watchlist, episode tracking — is stored exclusively on your device. The one exception is watchlist episode notifications, an opt-in feature that syncs a minimal, anonymous set of data so we can alert you to new episodes — see Section 02 and Section 05. The app displays third-party ads via Google AdMob — see Section 03 for full details.

01

Information We Collect

ShowAroo does not operate its own servers and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to any ShowAroo-owned system. Account creation is not required to use the App.

The one exception: if you enable watchlist episode notifications, your device syncs a small amount of anonymous data to our Firebase backend (operated by Google on our behalf) so we can check for new episodes and notify you. This is opt-in and off by default until you grant notification permission — see Section 02 for exactly what's synced.

If you sign in with Google, the following is accessed and stored locally on your device only:

  • Your display name
  • Your profile photo URL
  • Your email address (for account identification only)

If you sign in with TMDB, your session token is stored in your device's encrypted secure storage.

Additionally, the app displays ads served by Google AdMob, which may automatically collect certain device data as described in Section 03.

03

Advertising & Data Collected by Google AdMob

ShowAroo displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To deliver and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect the following data from your device:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID) — a resettable device identifier used for interest-based ad targeting and frequency capping
  • IP address — used to infer approximate location for regional ad serving and fraud prevention
  • Device information — device model, OS version, app version, and screen size
  • Ad interaction data — which ads are shown, viewed, or tapped within the app

This data is collected directly by Google LLC and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. ShowAroo does not receive, access, or store this advertising data on its own servers.

04

Interest-Based Advertising & How to Opt Out

By default, AdMob may serve personalised (interest-based) ads based on your Advertising ID and usage patterns. You can opt out at any time without any charge or loss of app functionality:

  • Android 12 and below — Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • Android 13 and above — Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • Google Ad Settings — Visit adssettings.google.com to manage or reset your ad preferences

Opting out does not remove ads from the app — it means the ads you see will be less tailored to your interests.

ShowAroo also provides in-app privacy controls depending on your region:

  • EEA, UK & Switzerland (GDPR) — A consent form appears on first launch asking whether you agree to personalised ads. You can withdraw consent at any time by going to Settings → Privacy & Ads.
  • US states (CCPA / VCDPA) — Tap Settings → Privacy & Ads → Ad preferences to access the "Do not sell or share my data" option.

These in-app controls are powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and take effect immediately — no app restart required.

05

Push Notifications

ShowAroo may send push notifications about new movie releases, upcoming titles, and trending content. Notifications are delivered via your device's built-in notification system.

Watchlist episode notifications are an opt-in feature: if enabled, we sync an anonymous device ID, a notification token, and your TV watchlist titles to a Firebase backend we operate, used solely to detect newly aired episodes and alert you. See Section 02 for full details on exactly what is synced and how to have it removed. No notification data is shared with third parties or used for advertising.

You can disable notifications at any time via Settings → Notifications or your device's notification settings.

06

How We Use Information

Sign-in information is used solely to personalise your in-app experience:

  • Displaying your name and photo on the Profile screen
  • Maintaining your login session between app launches

ShowAroo does not use your personal information for advertising, analytics, or profiling. Ad data collected by AdMob is used solely by Google for ad delivery — ShowAroo has no access to it. Data synced for watchlist episode notifications (Section 02) is used exclusively to check for and deliver episode alerts.

07

Data Storage

All preferences and activity data — watch history, episode progress, streaming service preferences, notification history, and watchlist — are stored on your device using local on-device storage.

Exceptions:

  • If you are signed in to TMDB, your watchlist is also synced to TMDB's servers and is subject to TMDB's Privacy Policy (see Section 08).
  • If you enable watchlist episode notifications, an anonymous device ID, notification token, and your TV watchlist titles are synced to our Firebase backend for the sole purpose of sending you episode alerts (see Section 02 and Section 08).

Authentication tokens are stored in your device's encrypted secure storage and are never transmitted to or stored by ShowAroo.

08

Third-Party Services

ShowAroo integrates with the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In Optional authentication. No personal data is transmitted to ShowAroo. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob Advertisement delivery and measurement. See Section 03 for full details on what AdMob collects. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase (Google LLC) Remote Config is used for feature flags, geo-availability, and update enforcement. The Google Play In-App Update API is used to prompt and enforce mandatory app updates. If you enable watchlist episode notifications, Firebase also provides: Cloud Messaging (delivers the notification), Cloud Firestore (stores your anonymous device record — notification token and TV watchlist titles), and Anonymous Authentication (assigns your device a random ID with no personal information attached, used only to keep your Firestore record separate from other devices). Network requests to Firebase may be logged by Google. policies.google.com/privacy
  • YouTube (Google LLC) Trailers may play in-app via an embedded player or hand off to the YouTube app. In-app playback may share request data with Google/YouTube. No personal data is shared by ShowAroo. youtube.com/t/terms
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) Provides movie and TV metadata, posters, and ratings. No personal data is shared with TMDB unless you sign in to a TMDB account, in which case your watchlist is also synced to TMDB's servers. themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
  • JustWatch via TMDB "Where to Watch" The app opens your external browser to show legal streaming availability powered by JustWatch data. No personal data is shared with TMDB or JustWatch by ShowAroo. justwatch.com/us/privacy-policy
09

Data Security

Authentication tokens are stored using your device's built-in encrypted secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Secure Enclave). We do not transmit sensitive data over unencrypted connections.

Since almost all ShowAroo data remains on your device, its security also depends on your device's own security settings — we recommend keeping your device updated and protected with a screen lock. The exception is the minimal, anonymous data described in Section 02 for users who enable watchlist episode notifications, which is secured by Google Firebase's own infrastructure and is never linked to your name, email, or TMDB account.

10

Children's Privacy

ShowAroo is not directed at children under the age of 13 and is not suitable for use by children. The app displays interest-based advertisements via Google AdMob, which requires users to be at least 13 years of age in most jurisdictions.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided information through the app or has been exposed to personalised ads, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11

Your Rights

Since ShowAroo stores almost all of your data only on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete your locally stored data — uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data (watch history, episode progress, streaming preferences, notification history, watchlist)
  • Turn off watchlist episode notifications — Go to Settings → Notifications to immediately stop future alerts. To request deletion of the anonymous backend record itself (notification token + synced watchlist titles), contact us via Profile → About
  • Sign out — clears your session and cached profile information from the app
  • Revoke Google access — via Google Account Permissions
  • Opt out of personalised ads — via your device's advertising settings or Google Ad Settings (see Section 04)
  • Withdraw GDPR consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland) — Go to Settings → Privacy & Ads to update your consent choices
  • CCPA opt-out (US states) — Go to Settings → Privacy & Ads → Ad preferences → Don't sell or share my data
